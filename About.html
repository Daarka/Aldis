Session VODs are processed using GMAssistant.app, a service that uses AI to transcribe recordings and consolidate them into summaries.

But wait! This is not a service we have used lightly, as we remain staunchly against generative AI here. The decision to use these tools came after a lot of combing through the singular developer's information pages, as well as speaking to them directly for clarification on some points. Their website addresses their AI stance here, but here are the reasons we saw fit to utilize the tools:
  • No training. GMAssistant does not use our provided data to train their AI, nor is it run through any third party utilities that would store it for their own use.
  • Utility-focused. These tools are not generative AI. They are not replacing human creation, but simply handling the tedious work of transcribing and synopsizing sessions that would have taken me countless days of daunting work to do myself.
  • Energy-conscious. GMAssistant is the product of a single developer, not a company. All processing is done on a computer they own that is powered solely by solar energy.
