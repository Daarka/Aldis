Said elf's approach was quiet--nearly silent. She barely sank in the snow as she moved across it, and so, with little forewarning, she was just suddenly on the edge of the firelight. She was similarly bundled up for the weather, a hood over her head and layers and layers of fabric around her gently stirred in the chilly breeze. But her face was bare; she was very fair in complexion, with smoky blue eyes and dusty brown hair, mostly kept neat in a series of braids. A gloved hand lifted to hook a finger in her scarf and tug it slightly away from her mouth--which was gently, placidly smiling. "Nils, I take it?"
